# Code of Conduct

We want Kedra to be welcoming to contributors of every background and experience
level. Participation requires respectful, constructive behavior.

## Expectations

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes and respect personal boundaries.
- Welcome questions and different levels of English proficiency.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, sexually harass, dox or publish private data.
- Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

These expectations apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and
other project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban
participants. Responses should be proportionate: clarification or warning for
minor incidents, restrictions or removal for serious or repeated violations.

The project uses <https://github.com/kedra-engine/kedra>. A dedicated
confidential maintainer contact is not established yet, and GitHub's private
reporting options have not been verified. Do not post sensitive reports in public.
Ask for a confidential contact without including sensitive details, and verify the
channel before sharing them. Platform moderation is not a substitute for a project
contact. Establishing that contact is an open governance task before wider outreach.

Maintainers should protect reporter privacy, avoid conflicts of interest and
explain enforcement actions to affected participants when safe to do so.
